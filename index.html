Find and remove your works from ao3.life

Three steps: find your works, gather their links, then send one removal email.

1Find your works

Have more than one pseud? Separate them with commas.

Write Explicit or Mature fics? On the ao3.life search page, set the Rating filter to Any rating before you copy the page. Otherwise those works won't show up in your results.

2Add your works

  1. On the ao3.life search page, select everything (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A on a computer; on a phone, long-press the text and choose Select All).
  2. Copy it.
  3. Paste it in the box below. Your works are picked out automatically.
More than one page of results? Repeat this for every page: open the next page on ao3.life, select all, copy, and paste here again. Your list keeps growing and won't add the same work twice.
Didn't work? Add links one at a time

On ao3.life, long-press one of your titles and choose Copy Link. Paste it here and tap Add. AO3 links and plain work ID numbers work too.

3Send the removal email

  1. Copy the email below.
  2. Send it from the email address on your AO3 account. That's how ao3.life checks the works are yours.
  3. Send it to legal@ao3.life.

Can't get into that inbox? Send it from any address and say so. ao3.life says they'll tell you what else they need. Once AO3 is back, you can also add a note to your AO3 profile for them to check.

This page doesn't contact ao3.life or store anything you type. Everything happens in your browser.
