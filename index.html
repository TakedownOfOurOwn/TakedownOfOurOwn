Find your works on ao3.life

Paste your AO3 username, work IDs, or full AO3 work links. This page builds links to ao3.life and drafts the removal email for you.

  1. Start with your username. It opens ao3.life's search in a new tab. The results show up there, not on this page.
  2. Skim for your name. The search also matches titles, tags and summaries, so a common username may pull in other people's works.
  3. Paste in the IDs you find. Each work ID links straight to that one copy, and every ID goes into the email.

Removal email

  1. Send from the email address on your AO3 account. That is the proof ao3.life asks for.
  2. If you can't reach that inbox, add a short note to your AO3 profile when AO3 is back, and say so in the email.
  3. Send to legal@ao3.life. One email can list every work.

This tool doesn't contact ao3.life or collect anything you type. It only builds links and an email draft in your browser.
