TakedownOfOurOwn · For AO3 authors

Find and remove your works from ao3.life

Three steps: find your works, gather their links, then send one removal email.

1Find your works

Have more than one pseud? Separate them with commas.

Write Explicit or Mature fics? On the ao3.life search page, set the Rating filter to Any rating before you copy the page. Otherwise those works won't show up in your results.

2Add your works

  1. On the ao3.life search page, select everything (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A on a computer; on a phone, long-press the text and choose Select All).
  2. Copy it.
